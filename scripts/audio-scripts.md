# Audio scripts — ready for ElevenLabs

> Generated from `src/data` (`npm run docs`). Use an **adult** voice (contest rule: no voices of people under 18).
> Export as MP3, ~30–60 s, and save to `public/audio/{id}_{lang}.mp3`. The site falls back to the browser voice when a file is missing.
> Optional per-part clips: `public/audio/parts/{vehicle}_{part}_{lang}.mp3` (text = the "Simple" explanation).

## Apollo 11 Lunar Module "Eagle" — EN  →  `public/audio/apollo11lm_en.mp3`

_117 words_

Once upon a time, a golden spacecraft with spider legs flew down toward the Moon. Its name was Eagle. Inside, two astronauts held their breath. Alarms blinked, boulders appeared, and the fuel was running low. Neil steered past the rocks, and a long probe touched the dust. Contact light! The Eagle had landed. Neil climbed down the ladder and took one small step. The two explorers gathered rocks, set up a seismometer and a little laser mirror. The next day, the top of Eagle blasted off and flew them home. The bottom half stayed behind, standing guard with its plaque: we came in peace. And every night, laser light from Earth still visits the mirror beside it.

## Pioneer 10 — EN  →  `public/audio/pioneer10_en.mp3`

_115 words_

Once upon a time, in 1972, a small probe with a big ear left Earth. Her name was Pioneer 10. She was supposed to travel for twenty-one months. She crossed the asteroid belt, where no one had gone before, and flew past giant Jupiter, taking its very first close-up portraits. She had no solar panels, because so far from the Sun the light is too weak. Instead, she carried warm nuclear hearts. Year after year, she whispered to Earth, a little more softly each time. After thirty-one years, her voice faded away. But she is still travelling, toward a red star called Aldebaran, carrying a drawing of us, and a map to find our home.
